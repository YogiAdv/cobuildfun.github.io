Alignment Session

Completed on Feb 3, 2026. These five answers define the team's focus.

1. What problem are we solving?

Hardware store owners in Tier-2/3 cities manually track inventory on paper or basic Excel. They lose money from stock-outs and over-ordering. They need a dead-simple digital inventory system that works on a phone.

2. Who is the customer?

Owner-operators of small hardware stores (1–3 employees) in Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian cities. Annual revenue ₹20L–₹1Cr. Currently using paper ledgers or nothing at all.

3. What is the MVP?

In scope: Add products (name, price, qty), update stock on sale, low-stock alerts via WhatsApp, basic sales log.
Out of scope (for now): Barcode scanning, supplier management, GST invoicing, multi-store, reporting dashboards.

4. How do we split equity and revenue?

Equal three-way split: 33.3% each (Priya, Arjun, Neha). Revenue share begins at first paying customer. Documented in collaboration agreement signed Feb 3.

5. What is our 8-week goal?

Ship a working MVP to 5 hardware stores in Indore (Neha's contacts). Get at least 2 to say they'd pay ₹500–₹1,000/month for it. Complete 10 customer interviews.
